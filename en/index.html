Asks before it acts

It checks every action against what you asked for and your rules. If they don't cover it, it asks. It never retries an action that stopped halfway on its own.

Has its own computer

It uses a browser on a private computer, separate from yours. You can watch what it does and take over when a page needs you.

Your passwords, in the vault

Passwords, codes and cards live in an encrypted vault. The agent uses them to sign in, but never sees them.

Remembers what you tell it

Install it in minutes

On your Mac, or on your own server so it works all day.

  1. Download Soul

    A file of about 500 MB. The first time you open it, it downloads its private computer, about 1.7 GB.

    Download for Mac
  2. Open it for the first time

    Drag Soul Agent OS to Applications and open it. If macOS shows a warning, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose “Open Anyway”. You only need to do this once.

  3. Choose its model

    Sign in with your ChatGPT plan, paste a key from your AI provider or use a local model like Ollama. Then say hello.

You need macOS 14 or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon, and about 20 GB of free space.

Straight answers

What does it cost?

Soul is free. You only pay for the AI model you use, directly to your provider. A local model costs nothing per request. Usage shows what it has spent so far.

Which models can I use?

Your ChatGPT plan, or a key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter and many more. Local models through Ollama work too. You can switch any time.

Which languages does it speak?

Soul replies in the language you write in: English, Spanish, Portuguese and many more. For now, the app's menus are in English.

Where does my data go?

It stays on the computer or server that runs Soul. Your AI provider receives what each task needs. When you connect apps like Gmail or Slack, that data passes through the service that connects them.

Can it make mistakes?

Yes, like anyone new on the team. That's why it asks before anything you haven't allowed, works where you can see it and logs everything. You can stop it at any time.

Does it work when my laptop is closed?

On your Mac it works while the app is open or tucked into the menu bar. If you quit it or the Mac sleeps, it waits. To keep it working all day, install it on a server.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. On a Mac it installs like any app and you talk to it like a person. We'll help you with the server.
