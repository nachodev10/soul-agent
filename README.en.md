## What it does

Soul is an agent with its own computer. It does your business's repetitive work and brings you the delicate parts ready to approve, in one tap.

- **Asks before it acts.** It checks every action against what you asked for and your rules. If they don't cover it, it asks.
- **Has its own computer.** It uses a browser on a private computer, separate from yours. You can watch what it does and take over.
- **Your passwords, in the vault.** Passwords, codes and cards are encrypted. The agent uses them to sign in, but never sees them.
- **Remembers what you tell it.** You can see, edit or forget each note, next to the message it came from.
- **Keeps watch for you.** Scheduled checks that only tell you when something changes.
- **One conversation.** The app, your browser, WhatsApp and Telegram all show the same conversation.

Some jobs to hand off: chasing late invoices, answering reviews, building the weekly report, sorting month-end paperwork, getting quotes from suppliers and turning email and Slack requests into tasks.

## Install on your Mac

1. Download [**soul-agent-os-arm64.dmg**](https://github.com/nachodev10/soul-agent/releases/latest/download/soul-agent-os-arm64.dmg).
2. Open it and drag **Soul Agent OS** to Applications.
3. Open the app. If macOS shows a warning, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**. You only need to do this once.
4. Choose its model: your ChatGPT plan, a key from your AI provider or a local model like Ollama. Then say hello.

You need macOS 14 or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon, and about 20 GB of free space. The installer is about 500 MB; the first time you open it, it downloads its private computer, about 1.7 GB.

**Want it working all day?** It can run on a server. [Write to us](https://github.com/nachodev10/soul-agent/issues/new?title=I%20want%20to%20install%20Soul%20on%20a%20server) and we'll send you access.

## Questions

**What does it cost?** Soul is free. You only pay for the AI model you use, directly to your provider. A local model costs nothing per request.

**Which languages does it speak?** Soul replies in the language you write in. For now, the app's menus are in English.

**Where does my data go?** It stays on the computer or server that runs Soul. Your AI provider receives what each task needs. When you connect apps like Gmail or Slack, that data passes through the service that connects them.

**Does it work when my laptop is closed?** On your Mac it works while the app is open or in the menu bar. If you quit it or the Mac sleeps, it waits.

**Can it make mistakes?** Yes, like anyone new on the team. That's why it asks before anything you haven't allowed, works where you can see it and logs everything.

**Why does macOS show a warning when I open it?** Because the app isn't signed with an Apple certificate yet. It's a one-time step.
